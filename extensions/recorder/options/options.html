Chatterbox Meeting Recorder

Before you connect or record

When you record, this extension captures your microphone and meeting audio, encrypts temporary audio chunks in this browser, and uploads them to the Chatterbox server you choose. That server decrypts them for storage and processing. Download a playable copy from your Chatterbox account after upload. The recorder key is encrypted in this browser. Your server operator controls data after upload. You must inform participants and obtain any required consent.
