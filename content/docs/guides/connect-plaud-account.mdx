---
title: Connect your Plaud account
description: Sign in to Plaud from Chatterbox, including the Google/Apple workaround.
---

Chatterbox signs into Plaud directly — the same way the official Plaud app
does. No screen-scraping, no headless browser, no reverse-engineered
mobile-app traffic. The flow you'll use depends on how you originally
created your Plaud account.

## Email-and-code (default path)

If you signed up to Plaud with an email address and password, you can sign
in from Chatterbox directly:

1. Enter the email address you use on [plaud.ai](https://plaud.ai).
2. Plaud emails you a 6-digit verification code.
3. Paste it into Chatterbox.

The code is forwarded to Plaud's servers and **never stored locally**.
After login, the access and refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM
before they reach the database. See
[Encryption at rest](../reference/encryption-at-rest) for what that
covers.

Plaud's account region (Global, EU, Asia Pacific) is detected
automatically: if the global endpoint responds with `status: -302` and a
regional API host in `data.domains.api`, Chatterbox follows the redirect
and stores the per-account region alongside the token.

## Signed up with Google or Apple?

If you originally tapped **Continue with Google** or **Continue with
Apple** on Plaud, the email-and-code flow will *appear* to succeed but
sync will return zero recordings. That's because the email/password
identity and the Google/Apple identity are two different accounts on
Plaud's side, even when they share an email address
([#65](https://github.com/riffado/riffado/issues/65)).

Real "Sign in with Google / Apple" inside Chatterbox is structurally
blocked by Google's authorized-origins policy on Plaud's OAuth client.
There are two workarounds.

### Easy path: the connector extension

Download the [connector browser extension](https://github.com/riffado/connector/releases/latest)
(AGPL-3.0) from GitHub and load it as an unpacked extension. The
connect screen surfaces a **Sign in with Plaud** button. You sign in to
`web.plaud.ai` the way you normally do; the extension forwards the
access token to your Chatterbox instance. Connector 0.2.4 or newer also forwards
a refresh token, which keeps you connected for up to 30 days. Older versions
connect for about 24 hours.

### Manual fallback: paste the token

If you can't or won't install the extension:

1. Open [web.plaud.ai](https://web.plaud.ai) in a tab and sign in with
   Google or Apple as usual.
2. Open DevTools (F12 or Cmd+Option+I) and go to **Application → Cookies → https://web.plaud.ai**.
3. Copy the value of the `pld_ut` cookie. A newly issued cookie is valid for 24 hours.
   Requests to `/device/list` and `/file/simple/web` use workspace tokens.
4. Open the **Network** tab and reload the page. Check the API hostname to find your region.
   `api.plaud.ai` is Global, `api-euc1.plaud.ai` is EU, and `api-apse1.plaud.ai` is Asia Pacific.
5. In Chatterbox, open **Paste token**, select the region, and paste the cookie value.

The pasted token is encrypted at rest the same way as a code-flow token.

## What about refresh tokens?

Email-code sign-ins have a 30-day access token and a 30-day refresh token.
When a refresh token is available, Chatterbox renews access in the background
until the original sign-in expires. Settings shows when the connection ends.
The dashboard warns you three days before that date and asks you to reconnect
when the sign-in ends. Older access tokens keep working until their own expiry.

## Switching accounts

You can disconnect or switch the connected Plaud account at any time
from **Settings → Plaud account**. Disconnecting deletes the saved tokens.
Previously synced recordings stay in your storage.
