Riffado Meeting Recorder

Connection

The easy way: open your Riffado instance, go to Settings → Meeting Recorder, and click Set up recorder. Or enter the details here.

Automatic recording

Choose what happens when you open a meeting on each platform. Auto arms the toolbar icon: one click (or the shortcut) starts recording immediately, and it stops by itself when the meeting ends. Prompt only notifies you. Chrome requires that one click; nothing can start capture without it.

PlatformModeSource

Keyboard shortcut: not set. Chrome may leave the suggested key unassigned; set it there if empty.

One-time confirmation

Automatic recording skips the per-recording checkbox. Confirm your recording policy once; the confirmation time is stored with every automatic recording.

Microphone

Grant microphone access once. By default the recorder uses your system default input, which is almost always the same mic your meeting app uses. Override it here only if your meeting runs on a different device.

Recording

Participant notice

Shown before a manual recording and used for the one-time confirmation above. Leave empty to use your server's text.
