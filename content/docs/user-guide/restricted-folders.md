---
title: Restricted folders
description: Choose an audience inside the Organization without publishing its contents.
---

A **Restricted folder** lives in the Organization tree and has an olive folder-lock icon. Everyone sees its name and position. Only its audience and curators can read its contents. This feature is available on shared self-hosted instances with a writable Organization.

## Create and file

Choose **New subfolder** in an Organization folder, turn on **Restricted folder**, and choose people by name or email. The creator is an audience manager and cannot be left out. You can select up to 20 additional people at a time. Curators always have access.

A restricted folder has no subfolders. Move existing subfolders elsewhere before restricting an open folder. Recordings, mail, tasks and agendas inside retain their owners. Only the owner edits restricted recordings, speaker names and summaries. The audience reads the owner's speaker names. Owner retention and processing apply.

## Request access

If you are outside the audience, opening the folder shows its name, its audience managers and **Request access**. You see no items, counts or other audience members. A pending request says **Request sent** and offers **Cancel request**. After a decline you can ask again.

Everyone in the audience receives an invitation and may approve or decline a request. Curators can decide requests through **Who can see it…**, but receive no invitation solely because they are curators. Approval grants access immediately.

## Who can see it

The folder menu's **Who can see it…** lists audience members, managers and people with items here, alongside requests waiting for approval. Any audience member or curator can add people. Only managers and curators remove them. They can remove anyone, including item owners. Before removing someone with items, a confirmation explains that their items stay here and the audience keeps reading them. The removed person keeps full owner access and can unfile or move their own items out without folder access. They cannot file anything new here and may request access again.

An item's header shows its folder path and **Visible to**, with audience avatars and **and curators**. An owner outside the audience sees a locked folder chip, **Visible to the folder's audience and curators**, and a remove action. Opening the chip leads to the no-access screen; audience identities stay hidden. Assigning a task to someone outside the readers shows a warning. You can add them to the audience or **Assign anyway**; assignment by itself grants no access.

## Restrict and open

A curator chooses **Restrict…** on an open Organization folder. The preview lists item owners who join the initial audience, items withdrawn from the Organization, earlier Almanac additions that stay and affected exports. Large folders show **Restricting…** while withdrawal completes. If interrupted, a curator can retry; the existing audience is kept and can be extended.

A manager or curator chooses **Open to the whole Organization…** to lift the restriction. The preview distinguishes items shared immediately from items waiting for their owner to finish speaker naming or other share requirements. Waiting items remain visible to their current audience. Cancelling their pending placement withdraws that filing. Organization retention starts after each item's 30-day grace; the preview shows its estimated end and items retention will reach.

Filing a restricted item into an open Organization folder asks **Share with the whole Organization?**. Speaker names and facts reach the Organization Almanac, and the item stays in the restricted folder too.

## The Almanac and copies

The Organization Almanac learns nothing from a withheld placement. Facts, speaker names and Learn stay with the item owner. Another open Organization placement can still publish the item normally. Opening the folder publishes through the normal sharing path. Restricting it again cannot recall people and things already added to the Organization Almanac, or copies people downloaded earlier. Curators can review earlier additions in **Almanac → Care → New from sharing**.

Organization folder exports skip restricted contents. Existing tracked copies are removed; pending or blocked removal remains visible in export settings and must be retried or repaired. Service accounts do not see restricted folders. Mail sent to the folder's address is accepted only from its audience. Owner backups retain restricted items and their placements; the Organization archive retains the tree and audience, its own items and published member items.
