---
title: Organization
description: Share recordings with everyone on your instance through one Organization folder tree.
---

A self-hosted Chatterbox can give its users a shared **Organization** next to
their private library. Owners choose an Organization folder while filing a
recording or mail. The item stays private while its processing and review gates
run, then is shared automatically. Everyone on the instance can open the
shared item; curators maintain its shared content. Nothing is copied: the item
and its source data stay with their owner.

## Turning it on

Set both variables and restart. On start, Chatterbox creates (or updates) one
**organization account** with these credentials.

```bash
SELF_HOST_MODE=shared             # the default
ORG_ACCOUNT_EMAIL=organization@example.com
ORG_ACCOUNT_PASSWORD=...          # 12+ characters, re-applied on every start
ORG_ACCOUNT_NAME=Acme             # optional, defaults to "Organization"
```

The email must not belong to an existing user; Chatterbox refuses to turn a
person's account into the organization account and logs why.

With [single sign-on](./sso), `ORG_ACCOUNT_PASSWORD` is optional: nobody
signs in as the organization account, which keeps owning the Organization
tree.

Removing the two variables makes the Organization **read-only**: what was
shared stays visible, nothing is deleted, and nothing new can be changed
except that owners can still withdraw their recordings. `SELF_HOST_MODE=local`
hides the Organization entirely.

Encryption at rest is unchanged: one instance key (`ENCRYPTION_KEY`) for all
data. The Organization is an access rule on top of it, not a separate
cryptographic domain.

## What people can do

| Action                                                    | Item owner | Member | Curator / organization account |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | :--------: | :----: | :----------------------------: |
| Choose or cancel an intended Organization placement       |    yes     |   -    |               -                |
| Withdraw a shared item                                    |    yes     |   -    |               -                |
| Open shared content; play or download shared audio        |    yes     |  yes   |              yes               |
| File a shared item in Organization folders                |    yes     |  yes   |              yes               |
| Edit shared content and complete its reviews              |     -      |   -    |              yes               |
| Manage Organization folders, profiles, people and things  |     -      |   -    |              yes               |
| Configure exports and back up the Organization            |     -      |   -    |              yes               |
| Rename, delete or erase the source item                    |    yes     |   -    |               -                |

Tasks are an exception to the shared-content rule. The home item's owner and
curators may edit, drop, close and merge a shared task; its assignee may also
close or reopen it.

Deleting an Organization folder never unshares anything: its recordings move
to the Organization root.

## Curators

With [authorization from the identity provider](./sso#authorization-from-the-identity-provider)
(`AUTHZ_MODE=idp`) nobody signs in as the organization account. People
holding `org.curate` (and `use`) curate the Organization instead, each
under their own name: on the Organization view of a shared recording
they rename it, transcribe it, summarize it, detect its topics, name its
speakers, run Learn and finish its review, and undo corrections. What
they create goes into the Organization's Almanac with them recorded as
its creator; the organization account stays the owner of the data, not
the author.

The server decides this from the curator's verified access, not from
the screen. Work started before their `org.curate` was taken away (a
summary, a transcription, a Learn run) checks again when it writes and
writes nothing once the right is gone. Curators also manage Organization
folders and profiles, people and things, suggested relations, exports and
backups.

Sharing and withdrawing remain with the item's owner. Managing Organization
AI providers requires `access.manage`, not `org.curate`.

## Two views of one recording

- **Private view** (opened from *Private*): the owner's processing result before
  sharing and again after withdrawal. The profile is resolved when the item is
  filed.
- **Organization view** (opened from *Organization*): the shared result that
  curators maintain. An Organization profile remains attached when one was
  used before sharing; otherwise the Organization folder default applies. If
  neither supplies one, the view waits for a curator to choose a profile.

An Organization profile may pin an Organization provider. Otherwise a manual
run uses the curator's default provider. A missing or disabled pinned provider
stops the step; Chatterbox never silently switches to another person's key.

Withdrawing or deleting an item removes it from Organization views for everyone
at once. Withdrawing a task's home item moves the home to the newest remaining
shared mention; without one, the task becomes private with the withdrawn item.

## Organization providers

Organization providers are AI credentials owned by the organization account.
Members can use them through profiles without seeing their keys. Only holders
of `access.manage`, or the organization account in local authorization mode,
may add, change, disable or remove them. Every management change is written to
the administrator audit log.

Each member may make 60 calls per hour to each Organization provider by
default. Set `ORG_PROVIDER_CALLS_PER_MEMBER_PER_HOUR` to change that limit; the
production Compose file must pass the variable into the application container.

## People and things

Speakers named in shared recordings become **Organization people**: one record
everyone names speakers with. Sharing a recording promotes every person
confirmed on it; a new name given in the Organization view is an Organization
person from the start. Suggestions and private notes are never shared --
notes stay visible only to whoever wrote them.

Everyone's [Almanac](../guides/almanac) shows their own people and things and
the Organization's. Curators change, merge or erase Organization people and
things, and those changes apply wherever the shared record is used. Anyone can
fold a private duplicate into an Organization record, keep private notes on
one, and give it nicknames of their own. The things and facts curators add are
the Organization's.

## Retention

- Each owner's retention settings govern their own transcripts and summaries.
- The organization account's settings govern the Organization view's
  transcripts and summaries.
- Audio is shared, so while a recording is shared it is kept until **both**
  the owner's and the organization's audio periods have passed; without an
  organization audio period, shared audio is kept. After a recording is
  withdrawn, its owner's audio period applies again after a 7-day grace.
- Removing originals from Plaud is always the owner's setting alone.

## Exports

As a curator or the organization account, open an Organization folder and add
a filesystem export as usual. It writes the Organization view of every shared
recording in that subtree, with audio read from each owner's storage. Curators
can also create an Organization backup; the archive records each item's owner.
